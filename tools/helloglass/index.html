The glass

Twelve dials for
one bend of light.

  • 01

    Refraction

    Light bends hardest at the rim, so the scene warps around every curve.

  • 02

    Chromatic split

    Red, green and blue part a little at the edges, the way thick glass does.

  • 03

    Frost

    Blur what sits behind the letters, anywhere from window-clear to sandblasted.

  • 04

    Rim and highlight

    A lit upper edge that returns faintly on the far side. Turn the light to any angle.

  • 05

    Tint

    Wash the glass in any colour, from a breath of blue to full stained glass.

  • 06

    Your scene

    Six drifting wallpapers, a solid colour, or drop in any image of your own.

How it works

  1. 01

    Type your words

    One per line. They write in, one after another, and the last one un-writes as the next arrives.

  2. 02

    Tune the glass

    Pick a pen font, a scene and the light. Everything updates live.

  3. 03

    Export

    One pass of the sequence, frame by frame, straight to video.
