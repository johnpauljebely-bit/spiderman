About

HI, I’MVANCE

A solo designer helping brands, creators and communities get logos, branding and visuals that don’t feel like a template.

Based in Vancouver, BC, and working with clients everywhere. Most of what I make is for Discord servers, ERLC servers and companies: identities that have to hold up across a whole community, not just one banner.

Every project is handled start to finish by one person, so the person you brief is the person who designs it, revises it and hands over the final files.
