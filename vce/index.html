About

Hi, I’mVance.

A solo designer helping brands, creators and communities get logos, branding and visuals that don’t feel like a template.

Based in Vancouver, BC, and working with clients everywhere. Most of what I make is for Discord servers, ERLC servers and companies: identities that have to hold up across a whole community, not just one banner.

Every project is handled start to finish by one person, so the person you brief is the person who designs it, revises it and hands over the final files.

    Legal

    The fineprint.

    The rules for ordering, and how this website handles your information. Last updated November 14, 2026.

    Orderpolicies.

    By placing an order you agree to everything below.

    1. 01

      Payment upfront

      Payment is due upfront, before any work starts. The amount due (in full or a deposit) is agreed when we scope the order. No payment, no slot: nothing is started, reserved or sketched until it’s paid.

    2. 02

      Robux only, via gamepass

      I get paid in Robux through a gamepass I set up for your order. I’ll add you as a trusted friend on Roblox first, so the purchase goes through without Roblox’s limits getting in the way. Payment through any other route isn’t valid, and I’m not responsible for Robux sent anywhere other than the gamepass I send you.

    3. 03

      No chargebacks, no disputes

      Once a payment is made and work has started, it’s final. Chargebacks, reversals and payment disputes after the fact aren’t accepted. If you pull a payment back, the order is cancelled, delivery stops, and any work already made stays mine.

    4. 04

      No middleman

      Orders are between you and me, directly. I don’t work through brokers, resellers or third parties handling payment or relaying messages for you. If someone else is paying for you, they deal with me directly.

    5. 05

      No bullshit

      Be straight about what you want and I’ll be straight about what it costs and how long it takes. Scope, price and timeline are agreed before payment, and that agreement is what I deliver. No endless scope creep, no moving goalposts, no ghosting.

    6. 06

      Revisions

      Revisions are limited to what we agree on in scope. Changes outside that scope are a new order and priced separately.

    7. 07

      Refunds

      Because work is custom and time is reserved for you, payments are non-refundable once work has started. If I can’t deliver what we agreed on, I’ll make it right or refund what I can’t deliver. That’s my call to make.

    8. 08

      Rights reserved

      I reserve all rights to everything I make, including drafts, concepts and unused work, and to my process, files and this website. A finished, paid order lets you use the final delivered files for the purpose we agreed on; it doesn’t transfer ownership of the underlying files or concepts. I may show finished work in this portfolio and on social media unless we agree otherwise before the order starts. You can’t resell, redistribute or claim authorship of my work, and I can refuse or cancel any order for any reason.

    Privacypolicy.

    How this website handles your information.

    1. 01

      The short version

      This site has no forms, comments, ads or tracking. I don’t ask for your name or email here, and I don’t collect or sell your personal data.

    2. 02

      What’s stored in your browser

      The site saves one small thing on your own device, never sent to me: a note that you’ve already seen the “click me” arrow on the home page, so it doesn’t show again. Clearing your browser data removes it. The site sets no cookies of its own.

    3. 03

      Hosting and server logs

      The site is hosted by a third-party host. Like any web host, it may automatically log technical details about visits, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and operation. I don’t use those logs to identify or track you, and I have no way to link them to who you are.

    4. 04

      Fonts, video and images

      Fonts, video and images on this site are served from the site itself, so loading a page doesn’t send your data to outside font or media providers.

    5. 05

      The tools

      The Ani Panel, Hello Glass and Patch Pixelizer are separate tools on this address, free for members of the Vance Collective Discord server. They ask you to sign in with Discord, which tells them only your name and whether you’re in the server; that sign-in is kept in your own browser for 5 days. The Ani Panel has its own privacy and terms page for what it handles.

    6. 06

      Links to other sites

      Some links, like the one to my Discord profile, take you to other sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies and I’m not responsible for what they collect.

    7. 07

      Commissions and Discord

      Orders happen over Discord, so what you share with me there (your Discord username, project details and payment messages) is covered by Discord’s own terms and privacy policy. I only use what you tell me to complete your order and keep a record of it. I don’t sell it or share it, except where I’m legally required to.

    8. 08

      Children

      This site isn’t directed at children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect personal information from them.

    9. 09

      Changes

      I may update these policies as the site or how I work changes. The date at the top of this page shows when they were last updated; continuing to use the site or placing an order means you accept the current version.

    10. 10

      Contact

      Questions about any of this? Message me on Discord.
